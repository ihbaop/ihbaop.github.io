bad news for her mother (01.03.2000)

Yes, I shall write it all down, you old cow, all: the first time, the last time, all the times in between, and then all the times I should have liked there to have been. I shall go on writing it down even out of habit, till there is nothing left to exorcise.
You may judge from that the emotional debt I feel your lovely daughter owes me.
(p.11)
B.S. Johnson, Poems Two
