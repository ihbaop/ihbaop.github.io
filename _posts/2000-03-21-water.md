<b> water </b>
<br>
<i>some seasons, swelling, pooling, wet / some seasons ebbing, buried, unseen</i>
<br>
<br>
<b> march/april </b>
<br>
<i>slushy thaw / cold and wet / hibernators stir / and long-distance migrants return</i>
<br>
<b> may/june </b>
<br>
<i> spring rains / pool and puddle / migration ends / and the land / comes to life </i>
<br>
<b>july/august </b>
<br>
<i> muddly land, humid air / water abounds / and life drinks </i>
<br>
<b>september/october</b>
<br>
<i>the land becomes / parched and / water is out of reach / for many</i>
<br>
<b>november/december</b>
<br>
<i>icy rain, first snow / the land becomes wet / just in time / to freeze once more</i>
<br>
<b>january/february</b>
<br>
<i>the ground turns / icy hard / life slows, but thrives / nonetheless</i>
